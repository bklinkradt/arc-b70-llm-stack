To find the arrival time, we need to calculate the travel duration and add it to the departure time.

### Step 1: Calculate the travel time
The formula for time is:
$$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$$

$$\text{Time} = \frac{312 \text{ km}}{96 \text{ km/h}}$$
$$\text{Time} = 3.25 \text{ hours}$$

### Step 2: Convert the decimal hours into minutes
Since there are 60 minutes in an hour, we multiply the decimal part by 60:
*   The whole number is **3 hours**.
*   The decimal part is $0.25 \text{ hours}$.
*   $0.25 \times 60 \text{ minutes} = 15 \text{ minutes}$.

So, the total travel time is **3 hours and 15 minutes**.

### Step 3: Add the travel time to the departure time
*   **Departure Time:** 14:05
*   **Travel Duration:** + 3 hours 15 minutes

Calculation:
*   $14:05 + 3 \text{ hours} = 17:05$
*   $17:05 + 15 \text{ minutes} = 17:20$

**The train arrives at 17:20.**
