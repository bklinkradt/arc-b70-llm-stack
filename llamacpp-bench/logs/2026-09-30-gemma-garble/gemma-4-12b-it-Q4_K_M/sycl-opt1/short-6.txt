### Translation
**"Das Wetter war schlecht, also blieben wir zu Hause und lasen Bücher."**

*(Alternative: "Das Wetter war schlecht, deshalb blieben wir zu Hause und lasen Bücher.")*

---

### Grammar Explanations

#### 1. Verb Position and Word Order (V2 Rule)
In German, the conjugated verb must occupy the **second position** in a main clause. 
*   In the first part of the sentence (*Das Wetter war schlecht*), the verb *war* is in position 2.
*   In the second part, when using the connector **"also"** (so) or **"deshalb"** (therefore), these words occupy position 1. Because the verb must stay in position 2, the subject (*wir*) must move to position 3.
    *   *Structure:* [Also] + [verb] + [subject]...
    *   *Example:* Also **blieben** (2) **wir** (3) zu Hause...

#### 2. The Simple Past Tense (*Präteritum*)
The sentence uses the *Präteritum* (Simple Past) to describe a completed action in the past. 
*   **war:** This is the past tense of *sein* (to be).
*   **blieben:** This is the past tense of *bleiben* (to stay). It is an irregular (strong) verb where the vowel changes from *ei* to *ie*.
*   **lasen:** This is the past tense of *lesen* (to read). It is also a strong verb where the vowel changes from *e* to *a*.

*Note: While Germans often use the Perfekt (e.g., "wir sind zu Hause geblieben") in spoken conversation, the Präteritum is standard for storytelling and written narratives.*
